What does this show?

This applet compares the binomial distribution to a normal approximation. Adjust the parameters to see when the curves align.

For X ∼ Binomial(n,p), μ = np and σ² = np(1 − p). Binomial bars are point probabilities; the normal curve is a density. To approximate P(X = k), use the normal area from k − 0.5 to k + 0.5 (continuity correction), rather than the curve height.
