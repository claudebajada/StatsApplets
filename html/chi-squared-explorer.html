Chi-Squared Goodness-of-Fit Explorer

What does this show?

This applet visualizes the chi-squared test for goodness of fit. Adjust category counts to see how the statistic and distribution respond.

χ² = Σ(Oᵢ − Eᵢ)²/Eᵢ, df = k − 1, and Cohen's w = √(χ²/N). Here the null probabilities are uniform and no parameters are estimated. w is a non-negative measure of departure from those probabilities; zero means an exact match and larger values mean greater departure. It is not bounded by 1. The observed bars are a deterministic teaching example, not a random sample. Counts are allocated by the largest remainder method after clipping and normalising weights; when N is not divisible by k, an exactly uniform integer allocation is impossible.
