What does this show?

This applet explores the independent samples t test. Adjust group parameters to see how the t statistic and p-values respond.

This is the pooled, equal-variance t test. The single SD slider specifies equal sample SDs s₁ = s₂ = s, so pooled sₚ = s. t = (x̄₁ − x̄₂)/(sₚ√(1/n₁ + 1/n₂)), df = n₁ + n₂ − 2. Signed Cohen’s d = (x̄₁ − x̄₂)/sₚ. Groups and observations must be independent; the exact test assumes normal populations with equal variances. The first plot shows illustrative models and the middle plot estimated uncertainty, not a second alternative sampling distribution. Choose the alternative before examining the data.
