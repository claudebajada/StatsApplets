What does this show?

This applet explores the one-sample t test. Modify the sample mean, size, and standard deviation to see the resulting t statistic.

The sliders specify sample statistics x̄, s and n, not known population parameters. t = (x̄ − μ₀)/(s/√n), df = n − 1; signed Cohen’s d = (x̄ − μ₀)/s. The first curve is an illustrative normal model; the middle curve shows estimated uncertainty centred on the observed difference. The null sampling distribution appears in the final plot. The exact t test assumes independent normal observations; small samples are sensitive to non-normality and outliers. Choose the alternative before examining the data.
