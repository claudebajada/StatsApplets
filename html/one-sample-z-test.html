What does this show?

This applet demonstrates the one-sample z test. Experiment with the sample mean and size to see changes in the z statistic and p-values.

The sliders specify the sample mean x̄, known population standard deviation σ and sample size n. z = (x̄ − μ₀)/(σ/√n). The normal model around x̄ is illustrative; the population mean is unknown. The z test assumes independent observations and a normal sampling distribution for the mean. The signed standardised difference uses known population σ. Choose the alternative before examining the data.
