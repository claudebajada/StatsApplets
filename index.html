Made for the teaching room

Bring a question, leave with a scenario.

Every explorer has presets, a reset button, a worked explanation and a shareable link. Lecturer mode adds an editable scenario title, student question and discussion notes.

  1. Choose an explorer and set up your example.
  2. Turn on lecturer mode and edit the guided question.
  3. Use student view to hide notes, then copy the example link into your slides.

Settings and notes are encoded in the URL. Answers are hidden visually in student view, not protected. No server stores the scenario.
